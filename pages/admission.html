The VedaKulam Fellowship

A rigorous 1-year program designed for students who want to integrate peak cognitive performance with deep emotional resilience. We select 50 students per cohort.

01

Application

Submit a detailed essay detailing your academic goals and the personal challenges you face regarding focus, burnout, or stress.

02

Screening (SASS)

Candidates undergo a preliminary Student Academic Stress Scale assessment to identify baseline cognitive baselines.

03

Interview

A 30-minute dialogue with our educators and wellness officers to align expectations and gauge commitment levels.
