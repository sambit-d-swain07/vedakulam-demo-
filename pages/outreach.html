Quantifying the Intangible

At VedaKulam, we do not rely solely on anecdotal reports. Our outreach wing is dedicated to conducting randomized controlled trials and longitudinal studies in academic environments to map the physiological and neurological benefits of 'Dhyana' (meditation) and 'Asana' (postures).

  • EEG Mapping: Analyzing brainwave states during deep focus modules.
  • Biometric Baselines: Tracking HRV and Cortisol levels in competitive exam aspirants.
